# Color Tokens & Material Meaning — Janani Craft

Every color in the Janani Craft design system is drawn directly from Bengal's traditional craft materials and geography.

## Color Tokens

### 1. Pora Mati (পোড়ামাটি — Terracotta Baked Clay)
- **Hex**: `#A84A28` (Dark Terracotta `#8B381A`, Soft Terracotta `#D97750`)
- **Significance**: Revered from the historic terracotta temples of Bishnupur, Kantajew, and ancient Mahasthangarh. Represents the sacred fertile alluvial soil of the Bengal delta.
- **Usage**: Primary brand accent, artisan authenticity seals, badge borders, call-to-action hover highlights.

### 2. Kansha & Pitoli (কাঁসা ও পিতল — Bell Metal & Antique Brass)
- **Hex**: `#B88E3E` (Light Brass `#D4AF37`, Muted Bronze `#8A6626`)
- **Significance**: Dhamrai's lost-wax metal casting heritage dating back over a thousand years.
- **Usage**: Luxury insignia, subtle hairline rules, metadata category tags, premium price indicators.

### 3. Bengal Neel (নীল — Natural River Indigo)
- **Hex**: `#192A45` (Midnight River `#111C2E`, Sky Indigo `#3A567D`)
- **Significance**: Natural indigo fermentation extracted from the *Indigofera tinctoria* plant, tied deeply to Bengal's historic textile resilience.
- **Usage**: Deep evening backgrounds, dark mode surfaces, high-contrast typography, hero narrative sections.

### 4. Dhaka Muslin / Kora Cotton (মসলিন ও কোরা সুতা — Ivory & Ecru)
- **Hex**: `#FAF7F2` (Warm Parchment `#F3EDE2`, Deep Ecru `#ECE5D8`)
- **Significance**: The legendary Dhaka Muslin, woven from *phuti karpas* along the banks of the Shitalakshya and Meghna rivers.
- **Usage**: Primary canvas background, card surfaces, image frame mats.

### 5. Charcoal Clay / Kali (কাঠকয়লা ও শিলালিপি — Deep Black Charcoal)
- **Hex**: `#1A1816` (Soft Charcoal `#2C2926`, Stone Gray `#4A453E`)
- **Significance**: Hand-ground lampblack ink used in traditional palm-leaf manuscripts and folk woodblock prints.
- **Usage**: High-contrast body text, primary dark buttons, framing lines.
