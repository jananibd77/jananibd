# Bengali Heritage Design System — Janani Craft

## Philosophy
Janani Craft is not a generic ecommerce template; it is a digital museum, cultural archive, and luxury boutique for authentic Bengali craftsmanship. The aesthetic blends:
1. **Ancestral Artisanal Legacy**: The sacred geometries of Jamdani looms, the running stitches of Nakshi Kantha, the warm baked clay of Bengal terracotta, and the radiant patina of Dhamrai brass.
2. **Contemporary Luxury Restraint**: Editorial pacing, museum-grade negative space, fine typography, and crisp non-rounded architectural geometry.

---

## Traditional Craft Pillars & Visual Languages

### 1. Jamdani (ডিম্বাকৃতি ও জ্যামিতিক নকশা)
- **Motifs**: *Panna Hazar* (thousand emeralds), *Jalar* (fringe lattice), *Terchi* (diagonal sprigs), *Bouti* (isolated floral stars).
- **UI Expression**: Hairline geometric grids, delicate gossamer borders, subtle dotted/dashed dividers representing weft-and-warp threads.

### 2. Nakshi Kantha (নকশিকাঁথা)
- **Motifs**: *Dhaner Sheesh* (paddy ears), *Chokh* (eye stitch), *Golap/Padma* (lotus flower), running stitch line work.
- **UI Expression**: Running stitch divider lines (`border-dashed border-stone-300`), rhythmic sequential cards, authentic narrative storytelling.

### 3. Pora Mati / Terracotta (পোড়ামাটি)
- **Motifs**: Carved temple friezes, earthen clay relief, warm sun-baked terracotta tiles.
- **UI Expression**: Deep terracotta color accents (`#A84A28`), tactile earthen card headers, clay-textured subtle badges.

### 4. Dhamrai Kansha & Pitoli (কাঁসা ও পিতল)
- **Motifs**: Hand-beaten metallic texture, burnished brass sheen, antique golden luster.
- **UI Expression**: Muted brushed brass badges, metallic borders (`#B88E3E`), gold foil inspired accents.

### 5. Bengal Indigo / Neel (নীল)
- **Motifs**: Botanical natural indigo dip, riverine midnight tones, historic Indigo revolt legacy of resilience.
- **UI Expression**: Deep moody navy/indigo dark accents (`#192A45`), contrasting hero backgrounds.

### 6. Dhaka Muslin (মসলিন)
- **Motifs**: Sheer transparency, unbleached organic cotton texture, ethereal lightness.
- **UI Expression**: High-contrast whitespace, ivory/cream warm canvases (`#FAF7F2`), delicate typography.

## Terminology & Cultural Respect
Always use authentic terms alongside English:
- *Jongol* / *Karigar* (Artisan / Craft master)
- *Tanti* (Weaver)
- *Mridshilpi* (Potter / Terracotta artist)
- *Kanshari* (Bell metal artisan)
- *Taant* (Traditional wooden handloom)
- *Anchal* / *Paar* (Textile pallu and borders)
